Founder & Lead Counsel

Sri Manoj Kumar Mohanty

Sri Manoj Kumar Mohanty, the Founder and Lead Counsel of MM Associates, is a distinguished and widely respected legal practitioner in Odisha. Enrolled with the Odisha State Bar Council in 1988, he has built an illustrious career spanning over four decades, marked by exceptional legal acumen, integrity, and professionalism.

He commenced his legal practice as an associate to Mr. Y.S.N. Murty, Senior Advocate, Orissa High Court, and within a short span, established his independent chamber — demonstrating remarkable initiative and capability early in his career. From 18 June 2000 to 1 July 2003, he served as one of the youngest Additional Government Counsel, representing the State before the High Court of Orissa with distinction.

With over 200 reported judgments to his credit, Sri Mohanty is recognized for his expertise across a wide spectrum of litigation and dispute resolution matters. His reputation for precision, ethical practice, and unwavering commitment to justice firmly establishes him as a leading figure in the legal fraternity of Odisha.
