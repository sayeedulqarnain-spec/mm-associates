Background & Details

Established in 1994.

Established in 1994, MM Associates is a distinguished boutique litigation firm founded by Sri Manoj Kumar Mohanty. Headquartered in Cuttack, Odisha, the Firm has a strong presence across the State, offering comprehensive legal services spanning litigation, dispute resolution, regulatory compliance, corporate law, and service law.

With decades of experience, MM Associates is defined by its unwavering commitment to integrity, excellence, and professionalism. Our client-centric approach, combined with deep legal expertise and a steadfast focus on quality, distinguishes us as a trusted and reliable legal partner.
